### I. Choose the correct answer

1. An aqueous solution of borax is
   a) neutral
   b) acidic
   c) basic
   d) amphoteric

2. Boric acid is an acid because its molecule (NEET)
   a) contains replaceable H⁺ ion
   b) gives up a proton
   c) combines with proton to form water molecule
   d) accepts OH⁻ from water, releasing proton.

3. Which among the following is not a borane?
   a) B₂H₆
   b) B₃H₆
   c) B₄H₁₀
   d) none of these

   a) Aluminium
   b) calcium
   c) Magnesium
   d) sodium

5. In diborane, the number of electrons that accounts for banana bonds is
   a) six
   b) two
   c) four
   d) three

6. The element that does not show catenation among the following p-block elements is
   a) Carbon
   b) silicon
   c) Lead
   d) germanium

7. Carbon atoms in fullerene with formula C₆₀ have
   a) sp³ hybridised
   b) sp hybridised
   c) sp² hybridised
   d) partially sp² and partially sp³ hybridised

   a) +4
   b) -4
   c) +3
   d) +2

9. The basic structural unit of silicates is (NEET)
   a) [SiO₃]²⁻
   b) [SiO₄]²⁻
   c) [SiO]⁻
   d) [SiO₄]⁴⁻

   a) SiO₂
   b) a chain unit of the form –Si(R)(O)(R)– (silicon bonded to two R groups and bridged by O, one O link)
   c) a chain unit of the form –O–Si(R)–O– (silicon bonded to one R group, bridged by O on both sides)
   d) a chain unit of the form –Si(R)–O–O–R (silicon bonded to R, two bridging O atoms, then R)

   a) Graphite

   a) Tetrahedral
   b) hexagonal
