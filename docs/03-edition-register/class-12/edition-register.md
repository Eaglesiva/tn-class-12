## Decision notes

- The source audit marks most official 2024 reprints as `SAME_VERSION_KEEP_EXISTING` because Page-2 imprint and page count match the existing main copy. Those books should remain on main; do not replace them with duplicate split sets.
- A smaller set of English Vol I books (notably Physics and Chemistry) has an official 2024 split on the update branch and is marked `VERSION_DIFFERENT; OFFICIAL_SPLIT_STORED_IN_UPDATE_BRANCH`. Review the exact row status and compare the direct Page-2 links before promotion.
- Full-book comparison is not complete for every same-imprint book (`FULL_DIFF_PENDING` appears in the manifest). Equal page count and imprint do not prove byte-for-byte or content equivalence.
- Page-2 imprint text is copied from the audit manifest. Direct Page-2 PDFs are linked for visual review. Do not mark CURRENT/ARCHIVE until the official school distribution status, whole-book content, TOC, and viewer routing are confirmed.
- No production book/page files were replaced.
