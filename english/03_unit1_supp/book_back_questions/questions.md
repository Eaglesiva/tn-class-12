### Warm Up (Page 30)

1. If you are punished for a prank your classmate played, how would you react to the situation?
2. While many would seek vengeance or feel sorry for themselves, some may put their trust in God, forgive others for the wrongs done to them and move on in life. What is your take on this? Discuss.
