### Warm Up (Page 40)

Based on your personal experience and knowledge, share your answers:
1. What is your favorite beverage to start the morning?
2. How do you prepare a cup of refreshing tea?
3. Discuss the cultural significance of tea drinking across different regions in India and the world.
