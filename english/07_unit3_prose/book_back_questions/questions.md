### Warm Up (Page 73)

1. Have you ever undergone physical pain or suffering? How did you respond to that experience?
2. How do you view suffering — as an obstacle or as an opportunity to build resilience and discover the true meaning of life?

3. **Listening & Speaking** (Boredom passage statements 1 to 3, Dialogue drafting on bank account, train cancellation, NSS camp, electronics shop, reading habits)
4. **Reading Comprehension** (The Cyborg Era questions a to j)
5. **Grammar** (Active to Passive Tasks 1 a–o, Passive to Active Tasks 2 a–n, Question Tags Tasks 1 & 2)
