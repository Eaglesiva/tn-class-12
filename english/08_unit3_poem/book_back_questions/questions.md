### Warm Up (Page 94)

1. This is the life cycle of a butterfly: Egg → Caterpillar (Larva) → Pupa (Chrysalis) → Butterfly (Adult).  
   Discuss with your partner the different stages in the growth of man from a newborn to an adult.
2. Words depicting different age groups (denarian, vicenarian, tricenarian, quadragenarian, quinquagenarian, sexagenarian, septuagenarian, octogenarian, nonagenarian, centenarian).
