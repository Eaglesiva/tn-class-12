### Warm Up (Page 101)

a) What qualities, do you think, characterise a gentleman? (External vs internal: well-dressed, courteous, honest, dependable).  
b) When we see a few coins scattered on the ground, we would probably pick them up on the sly or walk away ignoring them. What if it was an envelope full of Rs. 2000 bills? How are people likely to react in such a situation? Discuss with a partner.
