3. **Listening** (MCQs 1 to 6 on the story of the kite)
4. **Speaking** (Road safety measures & campaign slogans a & b)
5. **Reading** (Cyber Safety CBCID passage questions 1 to 6)
6. **Grammar: Subject-Verb Agreement (Concord)** (Task 1: Fill in blanks with correct option 1 to 10; Task 2: Error identification & correction 1 to 10)
7. **Writing: Describing a Process** (Task 1: Preparation of apple juice verbs completion; Task 2: Installing a computer sentence reordering 1 to 6; Task 3: 100-word descriptions 1 to 4)
