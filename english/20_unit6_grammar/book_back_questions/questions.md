### Scope & Structure of Master Grammar Revision

This comprehensive revision section covers the foundational grammar exercises for the entire Standard 12 syllabus across Units 1 to 6:
1. **Unit-1: Tenses** (Aptitude test passage error correction)
2. **Unit-1: Modal Auxiliaries** (Flooding news item completion with modal phrases a–g)
3. **Unit-1: Reported Speech** (Indirect speech conversions a–o)
4. **Unit-2: Prepositions** (Dolphins passage error correction 1–5)
5. **Unit-2: Conjunctions** (Spot and correct conjunction errors a–j)
6. **Unit-3: Active and Passive Voice** (Voice changes 1–20)
7. **Unit-3: Question Tags** (Tag validation and replacements 1–15)
8. **Unit-4: Kinds of Sentences** (Simple, Complex, Compound: Do as directed a–f & Sentence combining a–e)
9. **Unit-5: Non-finite Verbs** (Gerunds 1–10, Sentence unscrambling 1–5, Conversation completion 1–5, Gerund-to-infinitive rewriting 1–10, Error spotting 1–8)
10. **Unit-5: Articles and Determiners** (Fill in the blanks 1–15)
11. **Unit-5: Degrees of Comparison** (Sentence error correction 1–14)
12. **Unit-6: Spell Test** (Task 1: Correct spelling selection 1–22; Task 2: Editing passages 1 & 2 for spelling errors)
